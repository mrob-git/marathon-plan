## Execution quality

**Race (Sun 27):**
43:56, 4:24/km avg, HR 184 avg / 191 max. Sub-44 target hit. Full race effort, well-paced. The taper did its job.

**Easy runs (Wed/Fri/Sat):**
All three landed cleanly in Z2. Avg HR 136, 141, 138 — all below LT1 (148). Max HR touched 148 and 154 on Fri, likely hill spikes, nothing sustained. Pace discipline was good: 6:16-6:32/km range despite hilly terrain (46-58m elevation per run).

The standout number: Friday's 6:16/km at 141 HR. That's faster than most of August's easy runs (6:28-6:50/km at 138-142 HR). Mike noticed this himself — surprised at the paces for the heart rates. This is real. The taper + race stimulus has delivered a measurable aerobic efficiency bump.

No HR drift evident across any of the three easy runs — all steady state from start to finish.

**Mechanically:** Mike reported legs felt fatigued post-race and mechanics didn't feel great across all three runs. Peroneal tendon tender — attributed to carbon race shoes (narrow base, lateral load). Expected post-race residue, not a training concern provided it settles by mid-next-week in normal trainers.

No need to rush past it — the fitness comes from the sub-threshold sessions, not from mega long runs. Marathon-length long runs belong in the marathon-specific block later.
