## Data vs feel

Mike reported: energy 4/5, aerobically good, mechanically not great, peroneal settled, body otherwise good.

**The data confirms this exactly.** The aerobic efficiency gain is real and measurable — Saturday's 6:16/km at 141 HR is 20-25 sec/km faster at the same heart rate compared to August (when 6:40/km produced 139-142 HR). The engine is bigger than it was. The 10K race effort (43:56 at 184 avg HR) appears to have provided a genuine aerobic stimulus on top of the base work.

"Mechanically not great" is expected post-race. The legs did a hard 10K effort a week ago. Tendons, connective tissue, and neuromuscular coordination take longer to recover than the cardiovascular system. The aerobic system bounces back in 48 hours (HRV was 38 on Sep 28, back to 52-56 within two days). The musculoskeletal system takes 7-10 days. This gap — feeling aerobically sharp but mechanically rough — is a normal and expected pattern after a race effort.

**No red flags in the gap between data and feel.** They tell the same story.
